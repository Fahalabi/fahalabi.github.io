05 / Independent project · Validation stage

Scope-Safe Service Ops

I’m exploring how service businesses can keep customer-facing answers accurate while using AI to help organise the information.

I owned and directed the AI-assisted research and validation preparation: 30 Dubai home-maintenance and AC businesses researched, 10 public-source knowledge-gap snapshots, a discovery kit and a validation dashboard.

The working principle is simple: AI can extract, compare and organise. The business owner approves policy and customer-facing answers. This is a concept under validation, with no customer, pilot or revenue results claimed.

03 / How I work with AI

A useful assistant.
A human decision.

I use AI to make a first pass, compare information and test ideas. I check the source, challenge weak answers and review the work before it reaches a customer or a colleague.

In independent projects, I use the BMAD method to organise discovery, requirements and validation, with clear points for owner review.

  1. 01

    Understand the problem

    Start with the customer question, source material and the people who own the information.

  2. 02

    Draft, compare and challenge

    Use AI to organise the work, then look for gaps, unclear wording and unsupported answers.

  3. 03

    Test and review

    Check content against policy and try the journey from the customer’s point of view.

  4. 04

    Approve, publish and learn

    Keep approval with the right person, then use feedback to improve the next version.
